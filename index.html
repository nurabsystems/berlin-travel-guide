Welcome to Berlin

Berlin is a city where history meets innovation. From the iconic Brandenburg Gate to the street-art-covered remains of the Berlin Wall, every corner tells a story. This guide will help you plan an unforgettable visit.

Top Experiences

  • Walk through the historic Brandenburg Gate
  • Explore the museums on Museum Island
  • Taste authentic currywurst at a street stall
  • Cycle along the Berlin Wall Memorial
  • Relax in Tiergarten, Berlin's green heart
